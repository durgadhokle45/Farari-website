Ferrari in Motorsport

Ferrari's racing heritage is unmatched in the world of motorsports. With countless victories in Formula 1, endurance racing, and GT competitions, Ferrari's racing DNA is present in every road car they produce. The Scuderia Ferrari Formula 1 team is the oldest and most successful team in F1 history, with 16 Constructors' Championships and 15 Drivers' Championships to their name.

Ferrari's GT racing program continues to dominate tracks around the world, with the 488 GTE and GT3 cars winning major endurance races including the 24 Hours of Le Mans. The new 499P Hypercar marks Ferrari's return to top-tier endurance racing, already claiming victory at Le Mans in its debut season.

Ferrari Racing History

Seven decades of passion, innovation and unparalleled success in motorsport

1947

First Ferrari

The 125 S, Ferrari's first car, debuts at the Piacenza Circuit on May 11, 1947, driven by Franco Cortese. This marks the beginning of Ferrari's legendary racing heritage.

1950

Formula 1 Debut

Ferrari becomes one of the founding teams of the Formula 1 World Championship, the only team to compete in every season since the championship began.

1952

First F1 Title

Alberto Ascari wins Ferrari's first Drivers' Championship, beginning a legacy of dominance that would see Ferrari become the most successful team in F1 history.

1961

Sharknose Dominance

The famous Ferrari 156 "Sharknose" dominates the season with Phil Hill winning the championship. The car's distinctive design becomes iconic in motorsport history.

1975

Niki Lauda Era

Niki Lauda wins Ferrari's first Drivers' Championship in 11 years with the 312 T, beginning a period of renewed success for the Scuderia.

2000-2004

Schumacher Dominance

Michael Schumacher wins five consecutive Drivers' Championships with Ferrari, matching Juan Manuel Fangio's record and securing Ferrari's place as the most successful team in F1.

2023

Le Mans Victory

Ferrari returns to top-class endurance racing and wins the 24 Hours of Le Mans with the 499P hypercar, 58 years after its last overall victory at the legendary race.
